## Design Context

> Last verified against the codebase: julio 2026. The project runs the **Launch UI light warm professional** aesthetic on `main`; the prior dark-only Launch UI system, 8020 cream/sky-blue system, Cal.com, Stripe Press, and Apple-minimalism aesthetics are retired.

### Users

School directors, *sostenedores* (school network administrators), and decision-makers at Chilean educational institutions with 500+ students. They are evaluating Ethoz as a compliance and student safety solution. They browse on desktop during office hours, occasionally on mobile. Their job on this page: understand the product, feel trust, and schedule a demo. They are conservative buyers; trust and institutional credibility matter more than flash. Inspectors, teachers, counselors, and parents are secondary audiences reached through role pages and the product UI.

### Brand Personality

**Simple. Secure. Professional.**

Ethoz should feel like a competent institution, serious about data protection, approachable enough for a first-year teacher, and unmistakable when safety is at stake. The tone is **non-technical**: a school director should understand everything without Googling a term.

Emotional goals:

- **Primary:** "Trustworthy and they get me!" This was built by people who understand the reality of running a school in Chile.
- **Secondary:** Urgency, "The compliance deadline is approaching and we need to act"
- **Tertiary:** Relief, "Finally, a solution that handles the complexity for us"

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone:** Launch UI light warm professional. Warm off-white canvas (`#FAFAF9`), clean white cards (`#FFFFFF`), institutional blue accent (`#2563EB`), and a blue-gradient primary CTA. Generous whitespace, subtle warm-gray borders, one primary action per band. Inter Variable for all typography. The look is premium, institutional, and calm under pressure — designed for conservative buyers who equate clarity with trust.

**Why light:** The ideal client (directors and *sostenedores*, 40–60) associates dark-mode SaaS with technical/AI tools. A light, airy interface reads as institutional software: credible, familiar, and serious without being intimidating.

**Structural references:**

- Launch UI (launchuicomponents.com): light surfaces, blue accent, clean card hierarchy, conversion-focused sections
- Linear: restrained color, editorial hierarchy, information density without noise
- Stripe: credible, conversion-focused landing patterns

**Anti-references (explicitly NOT this):**

- **Generic AI slop:** The #1 enemy. No cookie-cutter layouts, stock photography, identical card grids repeated 4+ times, or anything that looks like a template with the logo swapped.
- Dark mode / near-black sections: The entire site is light. No dark bands, no charcoal cards, no mid-page inverted sections.
- Multiple accents: Blue is the only accent. No sky blue, no bright brand swatch, no pastel washes.
- Technical jargon: No "pgcrypto", "Row-Level Security", "pg_trgm" in user-facing copy. Keep technical terms for `/cumplimiento` and `/seguridad-datos` only.
- Data-dense dashboards: No Grafana/analytics feel.
- Cartoon illustrations: No playful edtech startup vibes. This is institutional software for people with real responsibilities.
- Heavy gradients and glows: Keep glows subtle and purposeful (blue glow behind hero mockup only). No neon, no rainbow gradients.

**Theme:** Light mode is the only mode. `color-scheme: light` is set on `html`. The `mode-watcher` package and any theme toggle have been removed.

**Key rules:**

- **One action color only.** Blue `#2563EB` (`--primary`) drives all primary interactive elements (CTAs, links, eyebrows).
- **Primary CTA is the blue gradient.** Hero/main CTAs use `bg-gradient-to-b from-cta-gradient-from to-cta-gradient-to text-cta-text`. Secondary/outline CTAs use `border-border bg-secondary/50` or `variant="outline"`.
- **Semantic colors only when meaning demands it** (destructive, warning, success).
- **NEVER hardcode colors** in templates; always use design tokens (`bg-primary`, `text-destructive`, `border-border`, etc.). Lint-enforced via `scripts/lint-hardcoded-color.sh` (use `// lint-ok` for legitimate SDK/raw-HTML exceptions).
- On primary tints (`bg-primary/5..20`), use `text-primary-active`, never bare `text-primary` (lint-tint-contrast.sh enforces).
- WhatsApp green `#25D366` is the only non-token color allowed (brand requirement for the WhatsApp CTA).
- Selection: blue highlight at 20% opacity (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`), foreground text.

### Typography (Launch UI: Inter Variable)

- **All typography: Inter Variable** via the `--font-sans` and `--font-heading` tokens. No DM Sans, no JetBrains Mono, no Cal Sans, no Playfair, no Newsreader.
- **Headlines:** weight 500, tight line-height (1.05–1.20), negative letter-spacing via `--tracking-display-*` tokens.
- **Body:** regular (400), line-height ~1.6, letter-spacing 0.
- **Hierarchy through weight and size**, not color. Headings stay `--foreground` (`#1C1917`). The blue accent lives in eyebrows, links, and accent words.
- **Eyebrow (section tag):** `.eyebrow` or `.label-mono`, mono-caps, primary (blue), uppercase. In Svelte templates the enforced convention is `font-mono font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.1em]` (lint-locked via `scripts/lint-eyebrow-tracking.sh`).
- `text-wrap: balance` on headings, `text-wrap: pretty` on paragraphs.
- All font sizes via Tailwind scale or mockup tokens (`text-mockup-xs`, `text-mockup-sm`, etc.), never hardcoded px values (lint-enforced via `scripts/lint-arbitrary-text-px.sh`).

### Spacing & Layout

- **Max content width:** `max-w-7xl` (80rem) for page containers
- **Section padding:** `section-editorial` utility (`clamp(48px, 6vw, 72px)`) for major sections; `pt-20 sm:pt-24` for first section after NavBar
- **Grid gaps:** `gap-6` for card grids, `gap-8` for step layouts
- **Mobile-first:** Design for 375px, add `md:` and `lg:` breakpoints
- **Consistent alignment:** NavBar, content, footer all align to the same `max-w-7xl` grid
- All spacing via Tailwind scale, never hardcoded values

### Radii: 16 cards / 12 buttons / 10 inputs / pill chips

Cards sit at 16px (`rounded-xl`). Buttons sit at 12px (`rounded-md`). Inputs and medium elements sit at 10px (`rounded-lg`). Chips and small pill markers go full pill. Hero mockup cards use 20px (`rounded-2xl`).

| Token | Value | Use |
|-------|-------|-----|
| `rounded-xs` | 4px | Badges, tight pill markers |
| `rounded-sm` | 6px | Chips, small markers |
| `rounded-md` | 12px | Buttons, nav pills, selects |
| `rounded-lg` | 10px | Inputs, medium elements |

### Components

- **NavBar:** Shared `NavBar.svelte`, fixed, `bg-background/80 backdrop-blur-xl border-b border-foreground/5`, Ethoz logo + Productos dropdown + CTA.
- **Footer:** Shared `Footer.svelte`, light background (`bg-background`), subtle top border, muted foreground links.
- **Cards:** `rounded-xl border border-border bg-card shadow-card-dark`, icon + title ALWAYS INLINE (same row), NEVER stacked vertically. No icon-in-colored-box wrappers (AI slop pattern, lint-enforced via `scripts/lint-icon-box-wrapper.sh`); use bare inline icons instead.
- **Buttons:** shadcn-svelte `Button`. Primary = blue gradient CTA (`from-cta-gradient-from to-cta-gradient-to text-cta-text`). One primary action per screen. Secondary/outline = warm borders.
- **Logo:** evolved interseccion / shield mark, two overlapping rounded layers; wordmark accent `z` is blue.
- **Icons:** Lucide via `@lucide/svelte`, size-4 to size-6. Default icon color: `text-primary` (blue) for action icons, `text-muted-foreground` for passive metadata.

### Content Principles

- **Non-technical language:** Write for a school director, not a developer. No database terms in user-facing copy.
- **Spanish-first, English-second.** All text via `t()` function, NEVER hardcoded in templates.
- **Canonical public URLs in Spanish** (`/como-contratar`, `/agendar`, `/funcionalidades/retiros-seguros`), using descriptive kebab-case slugs. English legacy slugs are redirect-only and must point directly to the final Spanish URL with a permanent 301.
- **No em or en dashes in copy.** Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead. This applies to all user-facing strings and marketing copy.
- **Ethoz COMPLEMENTS existing systems** (Napsis, Syscol, Lirmi); never say it reemplaza / replaces them.
- **Precise, authoritative, institutional tone.** Every word earns its place. No buzzwords, no fluff.
- **Show the model, not the price.** Consultation-driven sales, WhatsApp-first conversion.

### Accessibility

- **Target:** WCAG AA minimum, AAA where feasible
- **Contrast:** Foreground `#1C1917` on `#FAFAF9` exceeds AAA. Blue `#2563EB` on light backgrounds is AA for large text / UI components. Use `text-primary-active` on tinted blue surfaces for small text.
- **Motion:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (global rule in `app.css`)
- **Color blindness:** Never use color alone, always icons + text + color
- **Keyboard:** All interactive elements focusable, visible blue focus ring (global `:focus-visible` safety net in `app.css`)
- **Touch targets:** Minimum 44×44px on mobile
- **Warning surfaces:** Dark foreground text on warning amber; light text on warning amber fails contrast, never use it

### Design Principles

1. **Trust through restraint:** Every element earns its place. Whitespace IS the design.
2. **One color, maximum impact:** Blue for all primary interactive elements, one per screen.
3. **Non-technical by default:** If a school director can't understand it in 5 seconds, rewrite it.
4. **Nothing hardcoded:** All colors, spacing, and text flow through design tokens and i18n. Easy to edit later. No em or en dashes in copy.
5. **Light, calm, premium:** No dark sections, no charcoal cards, no mid-page inverted bands. Cards lift through subtle borders and soft shadows.
6. **Compact, not corporate:** Footer, nav, and UI elements should be efficient, not sprawling. Less padding > more padding.
7. **Accessible by default:** High contrast, keyboard navigable, screen reader compatible.
8. **Unified with the app:** Landing → `/admin` → Ethoz-app should feel like one continuous product. Never diverge tokens.
