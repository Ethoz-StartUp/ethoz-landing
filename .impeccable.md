## Design Context

### Users
School directors, *sostenedores* (school network administrators), and decision-makers at Chilean educational institutions with 500+ students. They are evaluating Ethoz as a compliance and student safety solution. They browse on desktop during office hours, occasionally on mobile. Their job on this page: understand the product, feel trust, and schedule a demo. They are conservative buyers, trust and institutional credibility matter more than flash.

### Brand Personality
**Simple. Secure. Professional.**

Ethoz should feel like a competent institution, serious about data protection, approachable enough for a first-year teacher, and unmistakable when safety is at stake. The tone is **non-technical**: a school director should understand everything without Googling a term.

Emotional goals:
- **Primary:** "Trustworthy and they get me!", This was built by people who understand the reality of running a school in Chile
- **Secondary:** Urgency, "The compliance deadline is approaching and we need to act"
- **Tertiary:** Relief, "Finally, a solution that handles the complexity for us"

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone:** 8020 design system, sky-blue vertical (8020IQ Brand Guide v1.1). Cream canvas (`#FAF8F5`), charcoal ink (`#18181B`), warm-white feature cards (`#F5F3EF`), and charcoal footer / dark bands (`#18181B`). Sky blue is the single accent. Generous whitespace, soft warm hairline borders, single primary action per band. DM Sans display typography paired with Inter for body and UI, JetBrains Mono for data and eyebrows. Light (cream) mode is the default page body; a charcoal dark mode exists for impact bands and premium surfaces. The deliberate dark surfaces are the **dark closing-CTA band** and the **charcoal footer**, stacked at the very end of each page; there are no other dark content sections.

**Structural references:**
- IntegratePIE.cl: Simple page structure, "¿Qué es?" and "¿Cómo contratar?" navigation, non-technical language
- Kimche.cl: Module-based product pages with "Productos" dropdown, WhatsApp-first sales, regulatory content as SEO
- Stripe / Linear / McKinsey publications: Restrained color, editorial hierarchy, information density without noise

**Anti-references (explicitly NOT this):**
- **Generic AI slop:** The #1 enemy. No cookie-cutter layouts, stock photography, identical card grids repeated 4+ times, or anything that looks like a template with the logo swapped
- Mid-page dark/inverted sections: No `bg-foreground` mid-page bands, no dark trust bars, no dark compliance/feature sections. The ONLY dark surfaces are the closing-CTA band + charcoal footer, stacked at the end of the page; never above the fold or mid-content
- Technical jargon: No "pgcrypto", "Row-Level Security", "pg_trgm" in user-facing copy. Keep technical terms for /cumplimiento page only
- Data-dense dashboards: No Grafana/analytics feel
- Cartoon illustrations: No playful edtech startup vibes. This is institutional software for people with real responsibilities.
- Gradients and glows: The 8020 spine is flat with soft warm shadows. One accent (sky blue). Reserve it. No glow effects outside the destructive safety signature.

**Theme:** Cream (light) mode is the default page body; the audience manages schools during daytime. A charcoal dark mode (8020 charcoal + titanium ramp) exists and is reserved for impact bands, premium surfaces, and the footer, not as a full-page user toggle.

**Key rules:**
- **One action color only.** Accessible sky blue `#0B72C4` (`--primary`) drives all primary interactive elements (CTAs, links, eyebrows). Reserve it; don't spray. The bright swatch `#0495FE` (`--accent-bright` / `--brand-accent`) is for fills, large display words, icons, and the logo only, NEVER for text or CTA labels.
- **Semantic colors only when meaning demands it** (destructive, warning, success)
- **NEVER hardcode colors** in templates, always use design tokens (`bg-primary`, `text-destructive`, etc.). Lint-enforced via `scripts/lint-hardcoded-color.sh` (use `// lint-ok` for legitimate SDK/raw-HTML exceptions).
- **Warning pairs with charcoal ink foreground.** White on amber fails AAA; don't do it.
- **Pastel badges are metadata-only** (`bg-badge-orange/pink/violet/emerald`), NEVER on CTAs (lint-enforced via `scripts/lint-pastel-on-cta.sh`)
- Platform brand exceptions: `--brand-linkedin`, `--brand-facebook`, `--brand-instagram`, `--brand-youtube`, external identity, do not repurpose
- WhatsApp green `#25D366` is the only non-token color allowed (brand requirement)
- Selection: bright sky highlight at 18% opacity (`rgba(4, 149, 254, 0.18)`), charcoal ink text

### Typography (8020 §08: DM Sans / Inter / JetBrains Mono)

- **Display family: DM Sans Variable** via the `--font-heading` token, used for ALL h1/h2/h3 + the `font-heading` class. True variable font (weights 100-900 + real italics). Display headings carry weight 700 (h2/h3/h4) to 800 (h1). Letter-spacing applied per display size via `--tracking-display-*` tokens (-0.0313em XL/LG, -0.0278em MD, -0.0179em SM). Confident, not decorative.
- **Body family: Inter Variable** via the `--font-sans` token. Body, buttons, nav, captions, labels.
- **Numerics + eyebrows: JetBrains Mono Variable** via the `--font-mono` token. Applied to `[data-numeric]` (tabular numerals) and to the mono-caps eyebrow / overline labels.
- **Fallback stack:** `--font-heading` falls back to `"DM Sans", "Inter Variable", "Inter", -apple-system, …`. Cal Sans, Playfair Display, and Newsreader have all been fully removed (lint-enforced via `scripts/lint-stale-fonts.sh`).
- **Headlines:** weight 700-800 (8020 authority is bold). Line-height 1.05-1.20 progressing by size.
- **Body:** regular (400), line-height ~1.55, letter-spacing 0.
- **Hierarchy through weight and size**, not color. Headings stay charcoal ink (`#1C1C1E`), never primary-tinted (the sky accent lives in eyebrows, links, and accent words).
- **Eyebrow (section tag):** `.eyebrow` or `.label-mono`, mono-caps, primary (sky), uppercase. The 8020 signature marker above headlines. In Svelte templates the enforced convention is `font-mono font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.1em]` (lint-locked via `scripts/lint-eyebrow-tracking.sh`); the CSS class itself uses the `--tracking-eyebrow` token (0.08em).
- `text-wrap: balance` on headings, `text-wrap: pretty` on paragraphs.
- All font sizes via Tailwind scale, never hardcoded px values (lint-enforced via `scripts/lint-arbitrary-text-px.sh`).

### Spacing & Layout

- **Max content width:** `max-w-7xl` (80rem) for page containers
- **Section padding:** `py-16 sm:py-20` for major sections, `pt-28 sm:pt-32` for first section after NavBar
- **Grid gaps:** `gap-6` for card grids, `gap-8` for step layouts
- **Mobile-first:** Design for 375px, add `md:` and `lg:` breakpoints
- **Consistent alignment:** NavBar, content, footer all align to same `max-w-7xl` grid
- All spacing via Tailwind scale, never hardcoded values

### Radii: 16 cards / 12 buttons / 10 inputs / pill chips

Cards sit at 16px (`rounded-xl`, the card radius). Buttons sit at 12px (`rounded-md`). Inputs and medium elements sit at 10px (`rounded-lg`). Chips and small pill markers go full pill. The hero mockup card is the only surface at 20px (`rounded-2xl`). No oversized curves beyond that.

| Token | Value | Use |
|-------|-------|-----|
| `rounded-xs` | 4px | Badges, tight pill markers |
| `rounded-sm` | 6px | Chips, small markers |
| `rounded-md` | 12px | Buttons, nav pills, selects (button base = `rounded-md`) |
| `rounded-lg` | 10px | Inputs, medium elements (input base = `rounded-lg`) |
| `rounded-xl` | 16px | Feature cards + standard cards (the card radius) |
| `rounded-2xl` | 20px | Hero mockup card (largest surface) |
| `rounded-3xl` | 24px | Reserved oversize surface |
| `rounded-full` / `rounded-pill` | 9999px | Avatars, status dots, pill chips, pill CTAs |

> Values map 1:1 to `--radius-*` in `app.css` (`xs 4 / sm 6 / md 12 / lg 10 / xl 16 / 2xl 20 / 3xl 24`). This is the project's authored 8020 scale; the codebase's `rounded-xl` cards were built for 16px.

### Shadows: soft and warm (8020 §10)

Soft, warm, low-opacity shadows. No hard or heavy drop shadows. `shadow-xl` / `shadow-2xl` are banned (lint-enforced via `scripts/lint-shadow-2xl.sh`), and neutralized glow utilities are blocked (`scripts/lint-glow-shadows.sh`).

| Utility | When |
|---------|------|
| `shadow-card` | Default card lift (soft warm) |
| `shadow-card-hover` | Hover state on cards |
| `ring-card` | Inset elevation for surfaces that need a subtle outline |
| `shadow-mockup` | Hero / product mockup card lift |
| `shadow-popover` | Dialogs, dropdowns |
| `shadow-glow-primary` | **Neutralized, no-op.** Utility retained for zero-file churn |
| `shadow-glow-destructive` | **RETAINED, safety signature.** Panic button, critical confirmations only |

### Textures (8020 §09)

Subtle background grids and dots at a 32px cell. Use one, never both, per surface. Dark-mode variants exist for charcoal surfaces.

| Utility | When |
|---------|------|
| `bg-grid-fine` / `bg-grid-fine-dark` | 32px grid texture on cream / charcoal surfaces |
| `bg-dots-fine` / `bg-dots-fine-dark` | 32px dot texture on cream / charcoal surfaces |

### Components

- **NavBar:** Shared `NavBar.svelte`, fixed, `bg-background/80 backdrop-blur-lg`, Ethoz logo + Productos dropdown + locale toggle + CTA
- **Footer:** Shared `Footer.svelte`, charcoal (`bg-surface-dark`), the deliberate dark surface at the page end. Light text via `text-on-dark` / `text-on-dark-soft`. NOT corporate.
- **Cards:** `rounded-xl border border-hairline bg-card` (warm-white on cream), icon + title ALWAYS INLINE (same row), NEVER stacked vertically. This is a hard rule, treat like an ESLint error. No icon-in-colored-box wrappers (AI slop pattern, lint-enforced via `scripts/lint-icon-box-wrapper.sh`); use bare inline icons instead.
- **Buttons:** shadcn-svelte `Button`. Primary = filled sky blue (`bg-primary`, white label). One primary action per screen. XL for hero CTAs. Elevation on hover comes from `shadow-card-hover`, not glow.
- **Logo:** evolved interseccion / shield mark, two overlapping rounded layers (charcoal + sky), paired with the DM Sans wordmark whose accent `z` is the bright sky swatch.
- **Icons:** Lucide via `@lucide/svelte`, size-4 to size-6. Default icon color: `text-primary` (sky) for action icons, `text-muted-foreground` for passive metadata.

### Content Principles

- **Non-technical language:** Write for a school director, not a developer. No database terms in user-facing copy.
- **Spanish-first, English-second.** All text via `t()` function, NEVER hardcoded in templates.
- **Canonical public URLs in Spanish** (`/como-contratar`, `/agendar`, `/funcionalidades/retiros-seguros`), using descriptive kebab-case slugs. English legacy slugs are redirect-only and must point directly to the final Spanish URL with a permanent 301.
- **No em or en dashes in copy.** Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead. This applies to all user-facing strings and marketing copy.
- **Ethoz COMPLEMENTA existing systems** (Napsis, Syscol, Lirmi); never say it reemplaza / replaces them.
- **Precise, authoritative, institutional tone.** Every word earns its place. No buzzwords, no fluff.
- **Show the model, not the price.** Consultation-driven sales, WhatsApp-first conversion.

### Accessibility

- **Target:** WCAG AAA for body text, AA for sky-blue interactive elements
- **Contrast:** Charcoal ink `#1C1C1E` on cream = AAA 16:1 for body. Sky-blue primary `#0B72C4` with a white label = 4.98:1 AA for buttons. Secondary text `#636366` is AA on cream. The bright swatch `#0495FE` is reserved for non-text / large-display uses where 3:1 is sufficient.
- **Motion:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (global rule in `app.css`)
- **Color blindness:** Never use color alone, always icons + text + color
- **Keyboard:** All interactive elements focusable, visible sky focus ring (global `:focus-visible` safety net in `app.css`)
- **Touch targets:** Minimum 44×44px on mobile
- **Warning surfaces:** Charcoal ink text on amber, white on amber fails AAA, never use it

### Design Principles

1. **Trust through restraint:** Every element earns its place. White space IS the design.
2. **One color, maximum impact:** Sky blue for all primary interactive elements, one per screen. The accessible `#0B72C4` carries text and CTAs; the bright `#0495FE` carries fills, icons, and the logo. Semantic colors only when meaning demands it.
3. **Non-technical by default:** If a school director can't understand it in 5 seconds, rewrite it.
4. **Nothing hardcoded:** All colors, spacing, and text flow through design tokens and i18n. Easy to edit later. No em or en dashes in copy.
5. **Flat, warm, clean:** No mid-page dark content sections (the dark **closing-CTA band + charcoal footer** at the page end are the deliberate exceptions), no gradients, no glow effects outside the destructive safety signature. `bg-surface-soft` warm-white or warm-white cards for alternation.
6. **Compact, not corporate:** Footer, nav, and UI elements should be efficient, not sprawling. Less padding > more padding.
7. **Accessible by default:** WCAG AAA. High contrast, keyboard navigable, screen reader compatible.
8. **Unified with the app:** Landing → `/admin` → Ethoz-app should feel like one continuous product. Never diverge tokens.
